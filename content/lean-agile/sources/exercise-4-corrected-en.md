Exercise 4 | Lean Metrics

Hout enzo B.V. produces wooden furniture for private customers. The production process consists of four steps: (1)
Sort material, (2) Assembling, (3) Apply lacquer layer, and (4) Packing and sending.
Use the accompanying process data sheet to answer the following questions. Assume one working day is 450 minutes
of available production time.
1. What is the takt time?
2. What is the average cycle time of each process step?
3. Which process steps do not meet the takt time?
4. What does it mean if the cycle time exceeds the takt time?
5. What is the lead time for each process step and for the entire chain? Use the simplified calculation convention in the
answer key.
6. What is the process efficiency?
7. How can process efficiency be improved?
