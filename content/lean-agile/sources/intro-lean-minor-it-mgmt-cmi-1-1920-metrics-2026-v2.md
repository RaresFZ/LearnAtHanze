## Slide 17

In process improvement, a SIPOC is a tool that summarizes the inputs and outputs of one or more processes in table form. 
The acronym SIPOC stands for suppliers, inputs, process, outputs, and customers which form the columns of the table

## Slide 19

Exercise
Delicious coffee is served daily in the 'bakkie leut' lunchroom. Customers can not only count on fresh coffee, made from fresh coffee beans, but also get a delicious biscuit by the coffee. Besides, of course, the usual sugar and milk that is served with it. This is not standard on the tables because of the freshness. 
The owner of the lunch room is busy setting up his process for serving customers smarter. Despite good service and satisfied customers, it does not seem to be organized just as well.
