## Slide 10

DevOps, What
28-10-2026
Titel presentatie aanpassen  10
Definition of DevOps
a set of practices, a cultural philosophy, and a combination of technical tools that unifies software development (Dev) and IT operations (Ops) to deliver software more quickly, reliably, and securely.
According to researchers like Len Bass, DevOps is defined as a set of practices that aims to shorten the time between committing a system change and putting it into production, while ensuring high quality. It is a combination of technical practices, cultural changes, and specific tools that integrates software development (Dev) and IT operations (Ops) to accelerate software delivery and improve reliability.

## Slide 11

DevOps, What
28-10-2026
Titel presentatie aanpassen  11
Key components
Culture and philosophy: 
Shared responsibility, communication, and collaboration between development and operations teams, breaking down the silos that once separated them. 
Automation: 
The use of tools and practices to automate tasks like building, testing, and deploying software, which speeds up the process and reduces errors. 
Continuous delivery: 
A key practice that enables frequent, high-quality software releases by integrating and automating the development and operations workflows. 
Feedback loops: 
Integrating mechanisms to gather feedback quickly and efficiently throughout the development cycle, allowing for rapid iteration and improvement.
