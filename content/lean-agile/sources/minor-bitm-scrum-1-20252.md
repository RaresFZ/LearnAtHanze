## Slide 7

Definition and History: Explanation of what Agile is and how it originated.
7
Definition: 
Agile is a project management and software development approach focused on flexibility, collaboration, and continuous improvement. It emphasizes responding to change, breaking down work into smaller iterations, and delivering value iteratively. This contrasts with more rigid, linear approaches like Waterfall.

## Slide 17

Itil (not in the Agile family!)
17
Information Technology Infrastructure Library, is a widely adopted framework of best practices for managing IT services. It provides a structured approach to aligning IT with business needs, focusing on efficiency, cost reduction, and improved service quality. Essentially, ITIL helps organizations deliver value to customers by guiding the design, implementation, operation, and improvement of IT services. 

## Slide 18

Itil vs. Lean:
18
ITIL and Lean are both frameworks used to improve IT service management, but they have different focuses. ITIL provides a structured framework of best practices for managing IT services throughout their lifecycle, while Lean focuses on eliminating waste and maximizing value for the customer. ITIL emphasizes processes and stability, while Lean emphasizes speed and continuous improvement.

## Slide 26

Sorting Game Setup
Teams of 4 or 5
Each team receives a Sorting Game template and a pair of scissors
Cut the template along the dotted lines: there are 72 cards and 3 column titles
Place the column titles on a table with space for the cards
Make a stack of the cards, shuffle them and place them top-down
SUPPLIES PER TEAM
Sorting Game template
Scissors
Stack of Post-Its
Pens

Sorting Game Round 1
This round has a fixed time of 3 minutes. Team members may not advise each other during this round
Each team member takes a card and places it below a colum title of his or her choice. Then the next team member takes a card
This repeats, until all cards are placed, or the time is over
Teams evaluate the results of another team and assign a number for 'right answers' (write on a Post-It)
Discuss the process

## Slide 29

10-9-2026

Sorting Game Round 2
Shuffle the cards, stack top-down. This round has a fixed time of 3 minutes. Team members may not advise each other.
Each team member takes a card from the stack and places it below a colum title of his or her choice, OR replaces a card that is already on the table to another column
This repeats, until all cards are placed, or the time is over
Assign points for 'right answers' to another team
Discuss the process

Sorting Game Round 3
This round has a fixed time of 3 minutes. Team members may advise each other . Cards are layed visibly on the table
Each team member takes a turn, takes a card of choice, discusses it with the team, and places it below a colum title of his or her choice
This repeats, until all cards are placed, or the time is over
