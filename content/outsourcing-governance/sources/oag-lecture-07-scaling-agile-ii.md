## Slide 5

SAFe framework basic components
Agile Release Train (ART): a number of teams (5 - 12) working on one product
Program Increment (PI): the incremental growing product of the ART
Frequency of a PI cycle: 4 or 5 two-week-sprints (8-12 weeks)
PI release: the shippable product of each cycle
System Demo: at the end of  the PI cycle the integrated product is reviewed

## Slide 6

SAFe framework
PI Planning Event (all teams!): a two-day event containing:
Business context presentation
PI objective roadmap
Team breakout: each team discusses the impact for their goals & plans
Teams present & discuss their plans
Final team plans
Risk management session
Inspect & Adapt workshop: product review 

## Slide 7

SAFe roles
Release Train Engineer (TRE): the coördinator of the ART, facilitates the ART meetings (like the team Scrum Master)
Product Owner (program level): the PO of the program
System Architect: ensures technical integrity, defines the architectural guidelines for the teams!
Scrum of Scrums Master: the facilitator of the team scrum master meetings

## Slide 18

S@S Roles
Scrum of Scrums Master (SoSM): facilitate teams
Chief Product Owner (CPO): coordinates the PO team
The Executive Action Team (EAT): facilitating teamclusters in the double scaled setting.  (CPO, SoSM, CEO, company managers) Goal is to resolve (escalated) impediments and take strategic decisions)

## Slide 19

S@S framework basic components
Scaled Daily Scrum (SDS): minimal one rep. per team
Scaled Retrospective: all scrum masters of each team
The Executive MetaScrum (EMS): a meeting of the CPO and the PO’s, prioritezes the backlog (! 1 Backlog for all teams!) , meet at least once per sprint.
