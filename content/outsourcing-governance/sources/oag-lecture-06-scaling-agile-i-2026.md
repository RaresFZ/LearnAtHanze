## Slide 4

Team Instructions
Work as a Scrum Team
The team appoints one Product Owner (PO)
The Product Owner communicates with the Space Force Coordinator (SFC)
Each team member has a different task and performs one step of the instruction-video and then passes the plane through to the next team member
After the Demo, all PO’s meet with the SFC for new instructions
Suggestions/requests for the SFC are allowed during the PO instructions

## Slide 5

Production cycle
PO instruction with SFC: 2 minutes
Sprint planning: 2 minutes
Building phase:  6 minutes
Sprint Demo: ca 3 minutes (all planes must fly 3 metres)
Team Retrospective: 2 minutes
5 Cycles!
